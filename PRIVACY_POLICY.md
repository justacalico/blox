# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Blox is an offline block puzzle game. This policy explains what the app stores
and how it treats your information.

## The short version

Blox collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no
network requests of any kind. Everything the app remembers stays on your
device.

## What the app stores on your device

The app keeps three pieces of data in local storage so your experience
persists between sessions:

- **Best score** — your highest score, so the game can show it to you later
- **Sound setting** — whether sound effects are on or off
- **Haptics setting** — whether vibration feedback is on or off

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted, shared, sold, or
backed up to any server we control. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## What the app does not do

- No personal information is collected (no name, email, location, contacts,
  or device identifiers)
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included
- No advertising or tracking of any kind
- No account creation or sign-in
- No third-party services receive your data

## Permissions

The release build requests no sensitive Android permissions. Haptic feedback
uses the device's standard vibration capability, which you can turn off in
the game's settings.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Nothing is needed
from a parent or guardian because there is nothing to consent to.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this document will be updated
in the repository and the effective date above will change.
